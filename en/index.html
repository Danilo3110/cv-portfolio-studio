CVS, ATS AND AI TOOLS

Clear to people. Readable by tools.

  • Clear sections and selectable text
  • Genuine skills and relevant terms from your profession
  • A file format that follows the employer’s instructions

An ATS manages job applications. It may extract CV details to fill a candidate profile. Complex tables, columns or an image-only document can make that import harder; parsing is distinct from a hiring decision.

Some AI tools, such as Workday HiredScore, help compare candidates with job requirements and prioritise applications. Their use depends on the employer and its process.

I focus on a clear, relevant and accurate presentation of your experience, using a format suited to the application.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before we begin.

Do I need projects for a portfolio website?

No. Your website is based on your CV and profession. It can present experience, education, skills, qualifications, services, a short biography and contact details. Projects and work samples are optional.

Which languages are included?

Both services include Serbian and English versions. A third or further language is agreed and charged separately; translation, language review, content and timeline are clarified in advance.

How quickly can I receive the work?

For the standard agreed scope, allow 1–2 working days for a CV or 2–4 for a portfolio. The start is confirmed based on availability and complete materials. A first website draft is planned within the first 1–2 working days; extra scope or delayed feedback can extend delivery.

What does the €249 starting price include?

One static portfolio page based on your CV and preferences, in Serbian and English, with mobile layouts, a contact form, SEO basics, initial launch and two consolidated revision rounds. More extensive content and additional languages are quoted separately.

Do I receive the code, and is hosting separate?

Yes, you receive source code and short instructions, and your domain and hosting account should belong to you. Domain, paid hosting and service subscriptions are not included in the creation fee. This is a personal presentation without a store, payments, user accounts or CMS.

Does a professional CV guarantee a job or an ATS pass?

No. The goal is a clear, relevant and readable CV that accurately reflects your experience. Skills and achievements are not invented. ATS systems and job requirements vary, so no pass, interview or employment outcome can be guaranteed.
